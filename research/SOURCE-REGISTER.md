- Current authoritative A-permit allocation/activation count matching the historical 35,558 claim.
- Final consolidated Georgian Tbilisi taxi annex with all changes; per-city Kutaisi, Zugdidi, Batumi and Rustavi applicability opinions and municipal clarifications.
- Airport-specific access/parking/commercial rights for this company at Kutaisi, Batumi and Tbilisi.
- Company-specific labour/agency/payment-regulatory/tax/insurance opinions and bank acquiring/payout approvals.
- Current bank/PSP transaction, foreign-card, payout, reserve and chargeback quotations; maps/messaging/cloud contracted prices at the expected usage mix.
- Interviews, observed eligible supply, driver work diaries, passenger conversion/retention, corporate commitments and bottom-up market sizing.
- Implementing accessibility standards and any transitional detail that applies to the actual launch date; regulator transfer mechanisms and DPO/DPIA conclusions for the exact processing design.

## Evidence maintenance

Store future evidence with owner, collection date, measured period, methodology, original URL/document/version, scope, approval status and next review trigger. Historical marketing statistics remain historical after a page changes. Recheck laws at legal sign-off and launch, vendor contracts before integration and public comparisons before publication. Unverified commercial assumptions remain visible in financial sensitivities until replaced by written evidence.
