## Verified public facts

### Municipal taxi scope changed in May 2026

[L1: current Georgian Licenses and Permits law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/26824), Articles 24(26¹) and 26⁵, now covers the capital **or another self-governing city**. The latter's council sets permit conditions, procedure and price; its designated municipal unit/public-law entity issues permits. The retrieved page is marked final consolidated; latest listed amendment overall is No. 1823, adopted 2026-06-25, published 2026-07-03. That metadata does not mean the July amendment itself changed taxi rules.

[L2: amendment No. 1584-Vმს-XIმპ](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6870591), adopted **2026-05-27**, published **2026-05-29**, changes the taxi clauses in Article 1(2)/(6). Article 2 makes it effective upon publication, **2026-05-29**, without a taxi-specific deferred commencement in this instrument. It establishes the wider framework; it does not document an issued driver/company permit or every city's implementing rules.

**Selected interpretation:** replace the generalization “taxi permits concern only Tbilisi” with this dated framework. Keep the legal classification and implementing act for each target jurisdiction explicit. A place named “city” is not by itself proof that it is legally a self-governing city; **Zugdidi's classification/applicability remains UNKNOWN in this review**. Do not extend Tbilisi categories to another municipality by analogy.

### Tbilisi categories: useful public text, incomplete current consolidation

[L3: council amendment No. 23-10](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5752819), adopted/published **2023-03-17**, generally effective **2023-04-01**; specified transitional provisions commenced **2023-03-20**. Its annex Article 11(2) restricts B-category pickup to the passenger for whom the driver came at the call location; 11(3) restricts taxi identifiers. Article 13(f) describes possible A-category commercial parking benefits, not automatic airport access.

**Selected direction:** app-booked service, with driver/vehicle permit eligibility checked against the actually applicable current rules. Treat B as a candidate for reviewed Tbilisi app bookings, not a permission already granted. Street hailing, A-only lane/parking benefits and airport rights require their own evidence.

**Access limit:** the [2018 parent resolution No. 24-80](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4275377) exposed its original 2018-07-27–2018-12-07 edition and required login/payment for consolidation. The 2023 amendment is not certified here as the complete October 2026 municipal rulebook; historical fees and permit terms must not be presented as current.

### Payments: one-sided agency and non-custodial technical support have specific limits

[L4: current Georgian Payment System and Payment Services law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1673253), No. 6304-Iს, adopted **2012-05-25**, published **2012-06-12**, effective **2012-07-01**. The accessed Georgian page is final consolidated; latest listed amendment is No. 1303-IVმს-XIმპ, adopted **2025-12-17**, published **2025-12-23**.

Article 2(ჰ³¹) defines a commercial intermediary acting only for the payer **or** only for the payee. Article 13(2)(ბ) excludes payments through that intermediary; 13(2)(ვ) provides a technical-support exclusion where transferred funds never enter its possession, excluding payment-initiation/account-information services. Article 15(1) limits payment-service provision to the listed eligible institutions/registered providers and statutory exceptions; the Article 13(1) service classification matters.

**Selected direction:** evaluate a disclosed transport-supplier/payee-only agency agreement and licensed bank/PSP settlement directly to approved beneficiaries; no wallet or stored balance. This is a proposed legal/business structure. A contract title or split API alone does not establish exemption, permitted custody or bank acceptance. Qualified review must map the actual obligations and flows to the cited provisions.

### Privacy: current Georgian law and two distinct amendments

[L5: current Georgian Personal Data Protection law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/5827307), No. 3144-XIმს-Xმპ, adopted **2023-06-14**, published **2023-07-03**. Free final consolidated Georgian text was retrieved; latest listed amendment is No. 1694, adopted **2026-06-10**, published **2026-06-15**. Articles 3–5 cover identifiable data, purpose/minimization/security and a justified lawful basis. Article 90(2) commenced the main provisions on **2024-03-01**; 90(3) commenced specified provisions including Articles 31/33 on **2024-06-01**. Controller/processor duties, retention, transfers, impact assessment and applicable DPO obligations need actual scope review.

[L6: June amendment No. 1694-Vმს-XIმპ](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6887031), adopted **2026-06-10**, published/effective **2026-06-15**. Article 1 changes Article 90(5): Article 6(1)(ს) commences **2030-01-01**. That subclause concerns special-category processing directly provided for under the **Public Procurement law**. This is not a postponement of ordinary research, geolocation, incident-data or ride-platform privacy obligations.

[L7: supervisory transition amendment No. 1289-IVმს-XIმპ](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6699148), adopted **2025-12-17**, published **2025-12-23**. Article 2(1)/(3)/(4) dissolves the former Personal Data Protection Service and transfers succession/case handling to the **State Audit Service from 2026-03-02**. Article 3(2) makes the substantive replacements effective then. Article 2(9) preserves applicable subordinate acts until replacement. Update authority references and verify applicable subordinate instruments rather than treating the institution change as abolition of privacy supervision.

**Selected direction:** complete purpose/data/recipient/retention/transfer maps using synthetic examples now. Actual interviews/diaries require the named protocol review, lawful basis, contact authority and restricted storage already specified in 01-01; public research does not satisfy those prerequisites. Never place raw identities/routes/incidents in research logs, event examples or backups by default.

### Tax: public guidance supports scenarios, not universal taxpayer treatment

[L8: Revenue Service small-business guidance](https://voice.rs.ge/Default.aspx?id=4236), undated live guidance retrieved 2026-10-04, describes **1%**, rising to **3%** when relevant annual gross income exceeds **GEL 500,000**, and status/eligibility obligations. It names a capital M1 taxi exception among restrictions on permitted activities. The page supplies no revision date establishing treatment after the May 2026 wider taxi framework. Do not assign small-business status/rate to all drivers or a platform company, or infer equivalent treatment for other cities.

[L9: Revenue Service VAT guidance](https://voice.rs.ge/Default.aspx?id=4331), undated live guidance retrieved 2026-10-04, states general mandatory registration when relevant taxable transactions exceed **GEL 100,000 over continuous 12 calendar months**, application within **two working days**, including the triggering transaction. It discusses **18%** VAT/reverse-charge examples and transaction corrections. This does not resolve whether platform revenue is commission or gross fare, individual supply exemptions, imported-service treatment or fiscal-document issuer.

**Access/decision limit:** the [Tax Code full-text endpoint](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717) failed retrieval because of content size. No historical PDF revision is substituted as current. Receipt/cash-register exceptions and issuer obligations remain UNKNOWN. Select separate transport and platform-commission accounting/receipt mappings for review; an app receipt is not automatically a compliant fiscal document. Keep calculator tax modes hypothetical until entity/status/supply/recognition and correction treatment are reviewed.

## Root follow-up: Zugdidi jurisdiction evidence

Additional primary sources accessed **2026-10-04** after the initial twelve-source review:

- [L13: Parliament resolution №987-IIს](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/3705170), adopted **2017-06-15**. Clause 7 merges the former self-governing city and municipality into Zugdidi municipality, with Zugdidi city as administrative centre; clause 9 commences on the day ordinary local elections are called. The exact election-call date was not independently retrieved here. This is a historical legal merger, not a taxi exemption.
- [L14: current Local Self-Government Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2244429), adopted **2014-02-05**, published **2014-02-19**; final consolidated page retrieved, latest listed amendment adopted **2026-05-27**. Articles 2(2) and 3 distinguish a single-settlement self-governing city from a multi-settlement self-governing community. No exact latest amendment publication date inferred from the list.

**Selected jurisdiction interpretation:** classify Zugdidi city separately from the merged municipality's service/administrative boundary; investigate the community-municipality model rather than copying a self-governing-city permit regime solely from its city name. These sources resolve the previously missing merger/definition evidence, while current taxi implementation, any subsequent status change and actual company/driver eligibility still require scope-specific review. An official municipal 2026 budget-draft search snippet corroborated the 2017 merger, but full PDF retrieval failed and is not used as independently inspected current law. No permission or professional opinion is inferred.
