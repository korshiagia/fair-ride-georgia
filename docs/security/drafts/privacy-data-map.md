## Provider lineage and evidence retention

[PP-G03/04 and PP-M02–06](../../../research/PUBLIC-PROVIDERS-2026-10-04.md) accessed2026-10-04 constrain DATA-01/03/08 **through every surface above**. Google service-specific terms modified2026-06-10 and Routes policy updated2026-09-28 have narrow permitted storage/display scope; a30-day lat/lng exception is not indefinite polyline/ETA/screenshot storage. Place IDs have a separate exception. Mapbox temporary geocodes/Search Box cannot become permanent route evidence; permanent geocoding eligibility does not grant other products or driver vehicle/offline redistribution rights. Own manual coordinates and approved own trip evidence are distinct from vendor-derived values even if numerically equal.

Proposed lineage record per field/object/event: origin class (customer/own verified/provider), provider/product/version, source ID/document effective/access dates, derivation inputs, purpose, licence/storage/display constraints, allowed retention/delete trigger, region/recipient, rule/version, authorized subject/resource and hold ref. Fail closed if provenance or allowed persistence is absent. Fallback changes only to independently licensed permissible inputs/output; never displays Google content with incompatible maps or archives vendor geometry simply for audit. Pricing must retain only permitted deterministic agreed fare facts to reproduce price independently of future vendor availability.
