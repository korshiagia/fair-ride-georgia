## State and authoritative evidence contract

Trip, assignment, payment-operation and safety states are independent. Completion does not mean capture or bank settlement. Every obligation binds city/jurisdiction, contract/beneficiary snapshot, fare/commission policy version and passenger-authorized amendments. Weak or stale location cannot alone trigger a wait/cancellation penalty.

PostgreSQL journals are immutable and balanced in integer GEL minor units, using an accountant-approved posting rule version and unique business effect reference. Corrections append reversing/adjusting entries; never edit history. The local command/intent + outbox is durable; WebSocket/Kafka delivery is not a durable money acknowledgment. Consumers deduplicate, provider operations use stable operation keys where supported, and status query + PSP/bank reconciliation recover uncertainty. **No exactly-once money guarantee spans database, broker and PSP.**

Do not post a confirmed capture/refund/payout merely on HTTP request acceptance, customer redirect or an unverified callback. Keep attempted/UNKNOWN state and approved pending entitlement separate; duplicate/replayed/out-of-order messages must not duplicate journal effects. No blind charge/payout retry with a fresh key after timeout. The operation type, same-key retention/replay semantics, lookup reference and finality window require provider evidence. Reconciliation owns unresolved exceptions and pauses affected payout/new collection until reviewed resolution.
