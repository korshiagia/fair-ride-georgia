## Driver interview script (25–35 minutes, parked/off-duty) — draft

1. Confirm approved notice/consent and right to skip. Ask city, broad zones, day/evening/weekend bands, category, owner/renter/fleet arrangement and single/multiple platforms; no ID/plate/permit scan.
2. Reconstruct a recent ordinary and difficult shift: online intervals including waiting, pickup and repositioning; offered/accepted/completed/cancelled counts; paid/pickup/unpaid/return distance; eligible fares, tips, funded incentives, deductions and payment mix. Mark recalled values self-reported and uncertain.
3. How do you allocate simultaneous app availability? Establish union of non-overlapping clock intervals and explain attribution; do not add each app's hours. What changes in quiet hours or another season?
4. Fuel/charging, tyres/maintenance, insurance/inspection, rent versus ownership/capital allocation, taxes/other costs: what period and original supporting record applies? Voluntary totals only; redact receipts before storage. Do not double-count loan principal and depreciation/lease, or empty mileage cost already in fuel.
5. Explain last cash commission collection, payout delay, refund/dispute and debt issue without customer identities. What remained owed versus actually received? Do not infer reviewed netting rights.
6. Describe pickup/no-show/arrival evidence, inaccurate location, fare change and support experience. Do not request incident victim identities or detailed sensitive events; offer approved separate support channel for distress.
7. What would cause you to try, retain or reject a service? Present 15% strictly as a hypothetical; ask what demand/paid-distance/payout reliability would make it worse despite a lower rate. Ask disconfirming questions, not a sales pitch.
8. Invite diary only under separate choice; agree safe later follow-up and stop if fatigued/distressed. Make no guaranteed income, airport access or service launch promises.

## Rider interview script (20–30 minutes) — draft

1. Confirm notice/consent; city, broad zones, resident/visitor and general frequency/time bands; no home/work address or continuous location.
2. Reconstruct the last successful and unsuccessful request: booking method, stated versus charged price, wait/ETA, pickup entrance/road-side confusion, cancellation and payment friction; no driver/contact identifiers.
3. What information made fare/deductions trustworthy? Which changes were explicitly agreed? How did a refund/appeal work? Preserve positive experiences and contrary evidence.
4. Discuss manual address choice, poor connectivity/battery, PIN assistance and readable information. Optional functional accessibility needs only; no diagnosis collection or safety guarantee.
5. Ask switching criteria and tradeoffs between price, reliability and support. Willingness-to-pay is stated preference, not sales/conversion. Distinguish hypothetical airport access from actual permission.
6. Summarize, let participant correct or withdraw, explain rights/follow-up as approved. Avoid detailed trauma/safety narratives in this economics study.

## Seven-day cost diary form — draft, restricted raw store only

One participant key linked separately to consent. Diary header: protocol version; city; broad zone/category; ownership arrangement; seven civil dates and timezone Asia/Tbilisi; season; owner; source-method tag. Daily shift row fields:

| Field group | Required values and validation |
|---|---|
| Time | Start/end intervals, breaks, union online hours including idle/pickup/repositioning; engaged hours separately. Multihoming simultaneous intervals tagged; explicit allocation method and uncertainty. No exact trace. Reject zero hours for hourly net, preserve zero-trip shifts. |
| Activity | Offered/accepted/completed/cancelled and cancellation reasons aggregated; weekday/weekend/day/night band; no trip/customer IDs in public output. Distinguish distinct offers versus repeated offers. |
| Distance | Paid km, pickup km, repositioning/empty return km, total working km; check sums. Home commute excluded or separately tagged. No route coordinates/addresses. |
| Money in | Eligible fare gross before commission, tips, funded incentives; card/cash totals, funds actually received, accrual/payable outstanding; currency GEL, policy/base and observed deduction including taxes where known. |
| Money/cost out | Commission basis/rate/amount; fuel/energy all working km; maintenance/tyres allocation; depreciation **or** lease/approved financing allocation; insurance/inspection; applicable verified taxes/other cost; original boundary ID, period, method, supporting restricted reference; cash collected/owed to platform separate. |
| Reliability | Observed/redacted record, self-reported recall or assumed allocation; missing fields, uncertainty/range, denominator, evidence reference and reviewer. Missing is not zero. Fuel purchases not automatically equal fuel consumed during a shift. |

Every cost appears once with canonical boundary and source period. Daily receipts may not repeat monthly insurance/rent in every row: allocate once with documented days/hours method. Net/hour = (eligible fares ×(1−effective commission) + tips + incentives − attributable costs)/union online hours. Report before/after uncertain capital/tax allocation and separate observed/self-reported costs. A diary is complete only with all seven days accounted for, including rest/no-work days; distinguish missing day from zero activity. Review overlap, cost allocation, totals and consent. Do not fill missing entries to hit quota.
